\# Customer Support Ticket Triage Environment



Real-world customer support ticket management for training AI agents.



\## Actions

\- \*\*prioritize\*\*: Set priority (1-5) for urgent tickets

\- \*\*respond\*\*: Draft response to customer

\- \*\*escalate\*\*: Send to management

\- \*\*resolve\*\*: Close resolved tickets

\- \*\*reassign\*\*: Transfer to specialist team



\## Tasks

\- \*\*Easy\*\*: Simple tickets with clear urgency

\- \*\*Medium\*\*: Mixed scenarios requiring judgment

\- \*\*Hard\*\*: Critical escalations and complex decisions
